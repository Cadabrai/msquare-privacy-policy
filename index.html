Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how mSquare Capital Management ("mSquare", "we", "us") handles information in connection with its internal messaging integration ("mSquare Integ"), which uses the WhatsApp Business Platform to send informational messages, such as research and news briefings, to recipients who have opted in.

Information we collect

  • Recipient phone numbers and WhatsApp profile names supplied by or for the recipient.
  • Message content we send and any replies the recipient chooses to send.
  • Technical metadata provided by Meta, such as delivery and read status and timestamps.

How we use information

We use this information only to deliver requested messages, maintain delivery records, and respond to inquiries. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Sharing

Information is processed by Meta Platforms, Inc. as the WhatsApp Business Platform provider and by service providers that help us operate the integration, under confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information where required by law.

Retention

We retain message records only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete them.

Your choices and data deletion

You may opt out at any time by replying "STOP" or contacting us. To request access to or deletion of your data, email investorrelations@msquarecap.com; we will respond within 30 days.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information.
